Credits / クレジット

Parallax & Sway uses original procedural geometry as its default scene. User-selected models, images and animations stay on the device; their authors' terms still apply.

Research / 参考文献

  • Ware, Arthur, Booth (1993), Fish Tank Virtual Reality — head-coupled display perspective.
  • Francone, Nigay (2011), Using the User's Point of View for Interaction on Mobile Devices — mobile head-coupled perspective.
  • Johnny Lee's head tracking demonstrations.
  • Sony Spatial Reality Display — inspiration for the experiment. This app uses motion parallax on a conventional display.
  • MediaPipe Iris — iris-based distance estimation context and limitations.
